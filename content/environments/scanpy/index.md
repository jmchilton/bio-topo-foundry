# scanpy

Scanpy and AnnData are the substrate the single-cell vertical assumes: `AnnData` is the object
that carries counts, embeddings, and annotations together, and Scanpy supplies the
preprocessing that comes before any geometry is learned.

Resolves from conda-forge, locked green.

# gudhi

GUDHI is the broadest of the general-purpose TDA libraries here: simplex trees, alpha and Rips
complexes, persistence, and representation utilities in one package. Several other fixtures in
this corpus depend on it rather than reimplementing complex construction, which makes it an
enabling dependency as much as a tool in its own right.

It resolves from conda-forge with no recipe required.

# scvi

scvi-tools learns a probabilistic latent representation of single-cell data. It is a companion
to the single-cell vertical rather than part of it: a comparison method that is not topological,
which is what makes it useful when the question is whether the topological scaffold earns its
place.

Resolves from conda-forge, locked green.

# phate

PHATE builds a diffusion-geometry embedding that preserves continuous progression structure
rather than only local neighbourhoods, which is why it is a natural comparator for the spectral
scaffolds in [[topometry-1.1-environment]].

One Bioconda package, locked green, and therefore an automatic BioContainer.

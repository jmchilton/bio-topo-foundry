# r-tdastats

TDAstats is the smaller, more focused R option next to [[r-tda]]: it wraps Ripser for the
computation and concentrates on visualization and permutation-based hypothesis testing over
diagrams.

The fixture stages an in-repo CRAN recipe with compiled code, verified building green on
linux-64, which holds it at L1.

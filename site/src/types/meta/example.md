# Example design record

The body says one thing about the Foundry's own machinery, in the voice its shelf calls for: an
`infrastructure` record describes what exists, where it sits, and when it runs, in the present
tense and without argument. `order: 1` is its place among the infrastructure records, not among all
records — the two shelves number independently.
